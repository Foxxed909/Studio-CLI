Frequently asked questions
What are credits?
Credits are consumed each time you send a message. One standard message costs 1 credit. Turbo mode (2.5× speed) costs 2.5 credits. Credits reset every billing cycle.
Do free models really cost nothing?
Yes — free models (GPT OSS, Llama, Gemma, Nemotron) can be used on the Free plan. You still spend 1 credit per message to prevent abuse, but the underlying AI call costs us nothing.
What is StudioTesting?
StudioTesting is an advanced developer environment for running batch prompt tests, comparing multiple models side-by-side simultaneously, and stress-testing AI behavior. It requires Dev plan or higher.
When will paid plans be available?
Paid plans are coming soon. Join the waitlist by sending an email to the team or following StudioCLI on GitHub. Free tier features remain free forever.
Can I bring my own OpenRouter key?
Absolutely. Add your sk-or-… key in Settings → API Key. Your own key bypasses the credit system and gives you direct access to all models you've enabled in OpenRouter.
